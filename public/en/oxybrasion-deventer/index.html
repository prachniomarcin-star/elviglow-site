Who is it for?

When can oxybrasion fit your skin goal?

Oxybrasion works mainly at the skin surface. At ElviGlow the goal is not to promise deep cleansing or dramatic regeneration, but to refresh the skin in a controlled, gentle way.

✦

Dull or tired-looking skin

When the main goal is a fresher-looking complexion without immediately choosing a stronger treatment.

✦

Slightly rough surface texture

For skin that feels a little uneven or lacks surface smoothness and radiance.

✦

A gentler first step

A useful option when you want to start calmly before considering more intensive skin-improvement treatments.

Treatment

What happens during oxybrasion?

Oxybrasion is also described as oxygen-water microdermabrasion: a pressurised oxygen-water or saline stream is used for superficial exfoliation rather than abrasive crystals.

01

Short skin check

We first check whether the skin is calm enough for treatment and whether a gentle surface treatment matches your goal.

02

Oxygen-water exfoliation

The skin surface is treated gently to remove part of the superficial build-up and refresh the complexion.

03

Calming finish

The treatment is completed with care chosen to suit the current skin condition.

Oxybrasion or cleansing?

Not the same goal as a cleansing-focused facial

If congestion, impurities and visible pore build-up are the main concern, a more cleansing-focused facial can be the more logical direction. Oxybrasion is positioned primarily as a gentle surface refresh.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know before your visit

How much does oxybrasion cost at ElviGlow?

The single treatment costs €59.

Is oxybrasion the same as deep cleansing?

No. Oxybrasion is mainly used for gentle superficial exfoliation and refreshment. A cleansing-focused facial may fit better when congestion is the main concern.

Can oxybrasion be done on irritated skin?

With active irritation, open lesions or a clear inflammatory reaction, we first assess whether postponing treatment is the safer choice.

Is oxybrasion the same as classic microdermabrasion?

No. Oxybrasion uses a pressurised oxygen-water or saline stream and is generally positioned as a gentler form of superficial exfoliation.
