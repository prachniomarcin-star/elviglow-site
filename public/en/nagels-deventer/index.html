Frequently asked questions

Good to know before your visit

When can I book a refill?

At ElviGlow a refill is possible up to 4 weeks after a new set. After that, a new set price applies.

Can I choose gel or acrylic?

Yes. For a new extension set, the choice is matched to the desired styling and natural nail.

Do you offer pedicure?

Yes. Classic pedicure is €40 and pedicure with gellak is €50.

How do I book enough time?

Choose the exact service in the appointment form so ElviGlow immediately knows the type of visit and approximate time needed.

Final treatment time can vary due to length, existing styling, nail condition and desired finish.
