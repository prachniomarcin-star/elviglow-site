Understand first, then choose

From a gentle refresh to more intensive regeneration

A facial treatment can mean very different things. That is why ElviGlow clearly explains what each treatment does and includes.

✦

Cleansing

For visible pores, impurities and skin that needs deeper but controlled cleansing.

✦

Glow and care

Texture and firmness

For a goal that goes beyond quick glow and moves more towards skin quality or regeneration.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know before your visit

Which facial treatment is good for pores?

When cleansing and impurities are the main issue, hydrogen cleansing may be a logical route.

What should I choose for quick glow?

Oxybrasion is the lighter option from €59. Glow Therapy with ampoule and mask is €89.

What if I have sensitive skin?

Tell us beforehand. The current skin condition determines whether treatment is adjusted or postponed.

How do I book?

The website prepares your request, but the appointment is confirmed personally via WhatsApp or Instagram.
