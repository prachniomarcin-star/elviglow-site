Understand first, then choose

One skin goal, different routes

Skin improvement does not automatically mean an intensive treatment. Sometimes cleansing or hydration is more logical; sometimes a regenerative treatment fits better.

✦

Dryness and dullness

Focus on comfort, hydration, glow and a calm skin barrier rather than continuously stronger active products.

✦

Pores and impurities

A deeper cleansing route can be more logical when the skin looks congested, coarse or uneven.

✦

Texture and firmness

When the goal shifts towards skin quality and regeneration, microneedling may become part of the plan.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know before your visit

What is the best skin-improvement treatment?

There is no single best treatment. It depends on what you want to improve and how the skin looks at that moment.

Do I need to book a series immediately?

No. For a first visit it may be smarter to see how the skin responds first.

Can I choose a treatment myself?

Yes, but if you are unsure you can choose advice / selection and decide the final direction in conversation.

Is this medical treatment?

No. ElviGlow provides cosmetic skin care and does not make medical diagnoses.

For persistent, painful or medical skin concerns, assessment by a doctor or dermatologist is more appropriate.
