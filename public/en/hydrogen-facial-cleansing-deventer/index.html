Who is it for?

When can hydrogen facial cleansing be the more logical choice?

This treatment is chosen when cleansing is the main goal. Not every dry or dull skin needs a stronger cleansing approach, so the current skin condition still comes first.

✦

Visible pores and congestion

When the skin looks more clogged or uneven and a light surface refresh does not match the main concern.

✦

Blackheads and impurities

When accumulated sebum, visible congestion or impurities are the main reason for booking a facial.

✦

Cleansing plus skin care

After the cleansing stages, the treatment is finished with oxygen infusion and an ampoule selected for the current skin needs.

Treatment

What is included in the €69 treatment?

The ElviGlow protocol combines several steps instead of treating hydrogen cleansing as a stand-alone device pass.

01

Cavitation peeling

A preparatory cleansing step that gently works on the skin surface before the main treatment.

02

Hydrogen facial cleansing

The central cleansing stage, focused on removing surface impurities and working on visible congestion.

03

Oxygen infusion with a suitable ampoule

The skin receives a finishing care step selected according to its current needs.

04

Manual extraction if needed

Added only when the skin condition actually requires it — not automatically for every client.

Comparison

Hydrogen facial cleansing or oxybrasion?

Oxybrasion is the gentler route when the main goal is surface refresh and a fresher look. Hydrogen facial cleansing is the more logical direction when pores, blackheads and congestion are the priority.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know before your visit

How much does hydrogen facial cleansing cost?

The ElviGlow treatment costs €69.

What exactly is included?

Cavitation peeling, hydrogen facial cleansing and oxygen infusion with a suitable ampoule. Manual extraction can be added when it is actually needed.

Is it the best facial for everyone with visible pores?

No. Visible pores can be influenced by several skin conditions. We first look at the current skin and decide whether cleansing is really the right direction.

Is this the same as oxybrasion?

No. Oxybrasion is positioned mainly as a gentle surface-refresh treatment, while hydrogen facial cleansing is more cleansing-focused.
