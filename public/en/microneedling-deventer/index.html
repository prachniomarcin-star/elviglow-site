Understand first, then choose

When can microneedling be a logical choice?

Microneedling is not a standard treatment for every skin. It may fit when the main goal is texture, pores and regeneration. If the skin is irritated or actively inflamed, a gentler route comes first.

✦

Visible pores and texture

✦

Frequently asked questions

Good to know before your visit

How many treatments do I need?

That varies by skin and goal. One treatment can be a good start; for texture or a longer plan, a series may make more sense.

How long can redness last?

Temporary redness or sensitivity can occur. The response differs per person and also depends on intensity.

Is microneedling suitable for every skin?

No. With active irritation, open lesions or an active skin infection, treatment is postponed.
