Frequently asked questions

Good to know before your visit

How long should the hair be?

Hair should be long enough for the wax to grip. If unsure, leave it and ask beforehand.

Can I exfoliate intensely the same day?

It is better not to add extra irritation around the waxing appointment.

What is the difference between bikini line and Brazilian?

Bikini line treats the visible border area; Brazilian is more extensive.

Can I combine multiple areas?

Yes, by agreement. Send all desired areas in one message so enough time can be reserved.

Waxing is not performed over damaged, strongly irritated or actively inflamed skin.
